# ADR draft review checklist

Review the draft before calling it ready for acceptance.

- Is the decision specific enough to guide future work?
- Does the context distinguish facts, assumptions, and unknowns?
- Are the alternatives real and materially different?
- Does the selected option state trade-offs as well as benefits?
- Are constraints and non-goals explicit?
- Are implementation implications evidence-backed or labelled UNKNOWN?
- Is verification observable, proportionate, and not falsely claimed as complete?
- Does a superseding decision preserve, rather than rewrite, prior history?
- Does the record avoid invented paths, versions, owners, dates, metrics, or test results?
